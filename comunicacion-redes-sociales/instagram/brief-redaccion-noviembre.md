## 4 · 11 noviembre — Gastro tendencia
**Serie: "Inspiración de otros sectores".** Formato: carrusel editorial.

**Concepto:** Café Leon Dore (Nueva York) — la cafetería dentro de la tienda
de la marca de moda streetwear Aimé Leon Dore.

**Fuente real, contenido propio de la casa (research interno TBNB, bloque
I+D en Drive) — citar o parafrasear:**
> "Café Leon Dore is not a coffee shop with a clothing store attached. It is
> a physical manifestation of a brand worldview, where coffee is the most
> democratic way to participate in it. That's why so many retail cafés fail
