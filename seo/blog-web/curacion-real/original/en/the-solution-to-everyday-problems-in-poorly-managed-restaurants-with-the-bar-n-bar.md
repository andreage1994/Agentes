#### Common Problems in Poorly Managed Restaurants

Many restaurants face similar problems due to lack of proper management. Some of these problems include:

Repetitive Errors: Without standardised protocols, the same mistakes are repeated over and over again, affecting service quality and customer satisfaction.

Lack of communication: The lack of a strategic plan and clear communication can lead to misunderstandings and operational errors.

Operational Inefficiency: Without efficient management, daily operations become chaotic and disorganised.

Staff Problems: Lack of proper training and high staff turnover can seriously affect the operation of the restaurant.
